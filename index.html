Read the head

What the numbers on a bolt mean

The grade is stamped on the head. The first number × 100 is the tensile strength in MPa. The second number is how much of that the bolt holds before it stretches for good.

8.8

800 MPa tensile640 MPa yield

The everyday high-strength bolt: steel structures, machinery, general fabrication.

Typical uses and sizes: Base plates, brackets, machine frames, general steelwork. In stock M6–M24.

10.9

1000 MPa tensile900 MPa yield

Stronger, for heavy machinery, cranes and joints that carry big loads.

Typical uses and sizes: Crane parts, heavy equipment, flanges, high-load joints. In stock M6–M24.

12.9

1200 MPa tensile1080 MPa yield

The strongest common grade: Allen bolts, dies, engines and high-stress parts.

Typical uses and sizes: Allen bolts, moulds and dies, engine and gearbox parts. In stock M6–M24.
