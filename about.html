A fastener shop that knows its sizes.

Advance Hardware opened in Sharjah's Industrial Area 2 in 1996. Since then we have supplied contractors, workshops, fabricators and other hardware shops with bolts, nuts, screws, washers and threaded bar.

We keep high-tensile bolts in grades 8.8, 10.9 and 12.9, the hard-to-find sizes, and the everyday supplies a job runs out of, like gloves.

Our team speaks Arabic, English, Hindi and Urdu, so you can order the way you talk. Send a list or a photo on WhatsApp and we reply with a quote in minutes.
