# Photo and video shot list: Advance Hardware

Real photos of our own stock only. No stock photos, no AI images.
Every shot replaces one grey placeholder in `media/` (the file name is given for each).

## Phone camera basics (read first)
- **Wipe the lens** with a soft cloth before every session.
- **One light from the side.** Daylight from the shop door or a window, or one desk lamp. Turn other lights off if they make mixed colours.
- **Black card behind small items.** A sheet of black card or a black cloth makes bolts and washers stand out.
- **Shoot at 4K** (video) and the highest photo quality. Turn on the grid.
- **Keep the camera still.** Lean on the counter, or use a cheap phone tripod. Hold your breath for the photo.
- **Tap to focus** on the most important part (the bolt head, the thread), then slide the brightness down a little so metal doesn't turn white.
- **No zoom.** Move closer instead. For macro, use the phone's macro mode if it has one.
- **Clean the items.** Wipe oil and dust off bolts before close-ups.
- Take **5–10 versions** of each shot. Send all of them; we'll pick the best.
